01 — The pub Refurbished 2018

A corner local, lighter than it was.

The Lord Napier has sat on the corner of Guanock Terrace for as long as anyone round here cares to remember. A proper street-corner local — not a gastropub, not a craft-beer pilgrimage. The sort of place you can drop into on the way home from the train, or settle into for the second half.

In early 2018 the room was opened up. Pillars came out, the ceiling went pale, the bar got a clean line of taps. It's a brighter pub now, but it's the same pub: regulars at the bar, coats over the chairs, and someone's dog under the table.

We're operated by Swan Inns and we keep the cellar honest — four ales rotating through the week, properly kept, properly priced.

02 — At the bar Cask & screen

Four guest ales. Two screens. One bar.
